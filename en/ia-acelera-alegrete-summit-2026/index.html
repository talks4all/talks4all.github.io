Alegrete Summit 2026

AI accelerates. Who builds the bridges?

Innovation, ecosystems and what got expensive once building got cheap.

When
29 October 2026, 4:15 pm
Where
Sicredi Essência headquarters auditorium, Alegrete, Brazil
Format
Talk in five acts, with eight questions for the room and clips, a fable, songs and websites made with AI
Audience
Entrepreneurs, companies, students, faculty and the innovation ecosystem of Brazil's western border region

About this talk

A lone programmer in Israel starts a side project and sells it six months later for US$ 80 million. A Swedish company makes US$ 400 million a year with 146 people. Building got cheap. The talk asks what got expensive.

Along the way: how fast adoption has been, what can already be built, including here, with this Summit's clip, fable, songs and website, all made with AI, and a real case: six students spent seven days on a task that a single student, using AI at a high level, delivered better in two hours. AI does not level the field, it widens the gap.

And also where it deceives: AI is not wrong in a way that looks wrong, it is wrong in a way that looks finished. At the end, the bridges: the local problem, the data only you have, a community that is ready and the base Alegrete already has to cross over.

In numbers

  • US$ 80 millionThe price of Base44, a lone programmer's side project, six months after it started.
  • 146 peopleLovable's team when it reached US$ 400 million in annualised revenue.
  • 128×Fewer hours to deliver more: 256 person-hours from six students against 2 hours from one student with AI.
  • 88% / 39%Of companies use AI somewhere, but only 39% see any effect on profit.

Running order

  1. Opening: the fable of Kauã

    Internet, a laptop and AI. The local problem is the edge: Kauã brings knowledge of the forest, AI brings the technique.

  2. Building got cheap

    Base44, sold for US$ 80 million six months after starting as one person's side project. So what got expensive?

  3. Act 1: speed

    53% adoption of generative AI in three years, faster than the PC and the internet. 88% of companies use it, 39% see profit.

  4. Act 2: what can already be built

    Lovable, Tractian, the Agricultural Engineering clip, alegrete.org and this Summit's website, all with lots of revenue or content and few people.

  5. Act 3: the difference is in who uses it

    34% more for novice support agents, 19% slower for experienced programmers. And the IoTEdu case: 7 days against 2 hours.

  6. Act 4: where it deceives

    The good-looking website that did not work, the agent that deleted a database, the invented case law. Speed without checking turns into loss.

  7. Act 5: the bridges

    What got cheap and what got expensive, cases from the Pampa, the community that saved lives in 2024 without AI, the BIA degree and the Summit Ideathon.

What you take home

  • Using it got easy. Turning it into value did not.
  • The edge is not having AI. It is knowing how to apply it.
  • AI does not level the field. It widens the gap.
  • AI is not wrong in a way that looks wrong. It is wrong in a way that looks finished.
  • The tool accelerates. A community that is ready is what saves.
  • AI lowered the cost of building. The territory decides what is worth building.
