AI in practice

How to use artificial intelligence to study, create and work better.

When
20 October 2026, 7:30 pm
Where
Colégio Emílio Zuneda, Alegrete
Format
A talk with four questions put to the room and real, unedited AI answers
Audience
Students and faculty of the technical programmes in Business Administration and Accounting

About this talk

AI is already in your day: in the feed that picked what you saw, in the instant payment approved in milliseconds, in the résumé a piece of software read before any person did. The goal tonight is to move from the passenger seat to the wheel.

First, understanding what it does well and where it fails badly, including inventing sources in perfect format. Then, learning to ask well: a five part recipe and four tricks, tested on a real retail task.

The second half is hands on: five uses to start tomorrow, from presentations and websites to videos, studying and the daily routine of accounting and management. Every example is a real AI answer, using fictitious data.

Running order

  1. AI is already in your day

    From your phone at 7:10 am to streaming at 9 pm, AI makes decisions for you all day long. Tonight is about taking the wheel.

  2. What it is, and what it is not

    A model that learned patterns from billions of texts and predicts the most likely word. Likely is not the same as right.

  3. Perfect format, false content

    The flawless citation of a book that does not exist, and why asking the same AI to check helps little.

  4. The golden rule

    AI writes the draft. You sign it, at school, in your internship and at work.

  5. Weak request, good request

    The same topic, cash flow, yields either a 400 word lecture or a table the bakery owner can actually use. The difference is context.

  6. The recipe and four tricks

    Who, context, task, format and limits. Then: have it ask first, ask for three versions, have it critique itself and compare two AIs.

  7. Use 1: presentations

    This presentation was made with AI. From zero to slide in four steps, with large fonts, little text and a test on the phone.

  8. Use 2: websites

    A city site, a lab site and the page of a bakery in Alegrete. Pretty is not the same as useful: useful comes from the request.

  9. Use 3: videos

    Script, slides, narration and captions. Two accountant videos made with AI, and one rule: never use someone else's voice or face without permission.

  10. Use 4: studying

    AI as a private tutor, not a cheat sheet. It removes friction, not effort.

  11. Use 5: accounting and management

    Bank reconciliation, spreadsheet formulas such as SUMIFS explained, minutes, difficult emails and 5W2H plans. The final number is checked in the spreadsheet, never in the chat.

  12. Precautions and five habits

    What never to paste into an AI, five habits to take home and a seven day challenge.

What you take home

  • AI writes the draft. You sign it.
  • The more context you give, the less it has to guess.
  • Every source, law and number that comes from AI: check it at the origin.
  • Ask for at least three versions and choose, instead of accepting the first.
  • Never paste personal data. Replace it with fictitious data.
  • Delivery got cheap. Judgement got expensive.
